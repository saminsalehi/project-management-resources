## Teaching: Project Manager Land
Samin created **Project Manager Land**, a YouTube channel launched in 2026 that explains project management in plain language for beginners, project coordinators, career changers and PMP candidates. Its lessons use simple worked examples, such as a small business website and a new hospital patient room, to show how goals, scope, schedules, people and risk fit together.

In "What is Project Management?", Samin explains that working on hospital projects shaped this view:

> "Project management isn't about having a perfect plan. It's about keeping people connected and helping them decide what to do next when things change."
