# Handover and Closeout Checklist

*Samin Salehi, PMP · Project Manager Land · Based on [What Is Project Management?](../guides/01-what-is-project-management.md)*

"Done" means the result is ready for the people who will use it, not just that the work stopped.

## Acceptance
- [ ] The result meets the agreed definition of "done"
- [ ] Required checks, inspections and tests are complete and recorded
- [ ] Outstanding deficiencies are listed with owners and dates
- [ ] The owner or sponsor has formally accepted the result

## Handover
- [ ] Users are trained, or a training date is set
- [ ] Manuals, warranties and as-built information are delivered
- [ ] Support or maintenance contacts are confirmed
- [ ] Access, keys and accounts are transferred

## Administrative closeout
- [ ] Final costs are reconciled against the budget
- [ ] Contracts and purchase orders are closed
- [ ] Project records are archived

## Lessons learned
- [ ] The team has reviewed what went well
- [ ] The team has reviewed what to change next time
- [ ] Lessons are recorded where future projects can find them
- [ ] The team and stakeholders are thanked
