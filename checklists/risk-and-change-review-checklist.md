# Risk and Change Review Checklist

*Samin Salehi, PMP ([ORCID](https://orcid.org/0009-0005-4143-2816)) · Project Manager Land · Based on [Risk vs. Issue vs. Change](../guides/04-risk-issue-and-change.md)*

Use this in your weekly project review.

## Risks
- [ ] Reviewed every open risk: is it still likely? Has its impact changed?
- [ ] Added new risks raised by the team, suppliers or stakeholders
- [ ] Every risk has an owner and a planned response
- [ ] Risks that have occurred are moved to the **issue log**
- [ ] Risks that are no longer relevant are closed

## Issues
- [ ] Each open issue has an owner and a target date
- [ ] Assessed the effect on later tasks: does it touch the critical path?
- [ ] Escalated anything outside the team's authority
- [ ] Told the people who depend on the affected dates

## Changes
- [ ] Every new request is logged as a change request, not just "added in"
- [ ] Impact on **scope, schedule, cost and quality** is assessed
- [ ] The decision is made by the person with authority
- [ ] Approved changes are reflected in the scope, schedule and budget
- [ ] The decision is communicated to everyone affected

## Guardrail
- [ ] No required inspection, test or safety check has been skipped to recover time
